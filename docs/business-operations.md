## SECTION 2: Missing Pieces – Actionable Checklists

---

### A. Legal & Business Structure

**Time estimate: 4–6 hours + 1–4 weeks for ASIC processing**

- [ ] Register as Sole Trader OR Pty Ltd via ASIC Connect (https://connectonline.asic.gov.au)
  - **Recommended: Pty Ltd** for liability protection and distributor credibility
  - Cost: $538 for Pty Ltd registration; $44–102 for business name
  - First step: Go to business.gov.au/registrations – takes 5 min to get ABN
- [ ] Complete IP Australia trademark search for "Neural Hardware"
  - URL: https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au
  - Cost: Free to search; $250/class to register (do this in Month 2)
  - First step: Search "Neural Hardware" in Australian Trade Mark Search (ATMOSS)
- [ ] Arrange business insurance:
  - [ ] Public Liability ($10M minimum): ~$400–800/yr via BizCover (https://www.bizcover.com.au)
  - [ ] Product Liability: Often bundled with public liability
  - [ ] Cyber Insurance: ~$500–1,200/yr (critical for e-commerce)
- [ ] Draft Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy:
  - Quick start: Use Shopify's templates then add ACL clauses
  - Budget option: LegalVision template $99 (https://legalvision.com.au)
  - First step: Generate from Shopify Settings → Legal, then customise

### C. Financial Infrastructure

**Time estimate: 3–5 hours; some items take 1–5 business days**

- [ ] Open dedicated business bank account:
  - **Recommended: NAB Business Everyday Account** (no monthly fee under $3k turnover)
  - OR: Wise Business (multi-currency, excellent for China payments)
  - First step: Visit nab.com.au/business → Online application (~30 min)

- [ ] Set up accounting software:
  - **Xero** ($32/mo "Starter" plan) – connects to NAB/ANZ automatically
  - First step: Sign up at xero.com/au; connect bank in Settings → Bank Accounts
  - Set up: Chart of accounts for e-commerce (COGS, postage, advertising, platform fees)

- [ ] GST registration:
  - **Required once you expect $75k+ annual turnover** (register early for professional appearance)
  - Register at ATO Business Portal: https://www.ato.gov.au/business
  - First step: Log in with myGovID → Register for GST
  - BAS lodgement: Quarterly; use Xero to auto-calculate

- [ ] Set up business credit card:
  - American Express Business Explorer Card (55-day interest free, bonus points on tech spend)
  - OR: NAB Business Card (lower fees)
  - First step: Check eligibility; apply after ABN is registered

- [ ] Cash reserve target:
  - **Month 1–3:** $5,000 minimum (covers first 2 inventory orders + ad spend)
  - **Month 4–6:** $15,000 (covers 30-day accounts payable + 60 days ad spend)
  - **Month 7–12:** $30,000 (covers Malaysia 4PL deposit + bulk order)

- [ ] Set up PayPal Business account:
  - https://www.paypal.com/au/business
  - Link business bank account; verify email; enable seller protection

### E. Operational Processes

**Time estimate: 4–6 hours to set up templates**

- [ ] Returns workflow:
  1. Customer fills returns form (Google Form or Gorgias ticket)
  2. Founder/VA reviews against ACL grounds (major/minor fault vs change-of-mind)
  3. If approved: Email prepaid return label (Australia Post) OR customer pays for CoM
  4. On receipt: Inspect; process Shopify refund or replacement within 2 business days
  5. First step: Create a Google Form; embed on Shopify Returns page

- [ ] Warranty claims process:
  1. Customer emails support@neuralhardware.com.au with proof of purchase + description of fault
  2. VA logs in Gorgias ticket; assigns to Founder for assessment
  3. Founder contacts distributor's warranty department (e.g., Dicker Data warranty team)
  4. Resolution: Repair/replacement within warranty terms
  5. Create template email responses (see below)

- [ ] Customer service email templates (save in Gorgias or Google Docs):
  - Order confirmation follow-up: "Thanks for your order! Here's your tracking: [X]"
  - Shipping delay: "We're sorry for the delay — your order is now expected [date]"
  - Warranty initiation: "We've received your warranty claim. Our team will respond within 1 business day."
  - ACL refund: "Under the Australian Consumer Law, we are issuing a full refund of $[X]. This will appear in 3–5 business days."
  - Change-of-mind return: "We're happy to accept your return. Please note: return postage is at your cost for change-of-mind returns."

- [ ] Stock reconciliation:
  - Weekly: Compare Shopify inventory vs distributor's available stock (download CSV from Dicker Data portal)
  - Tool: Shopify inventory export + Google Sheets comparison formula
  - Monthly: Reconcile with Xero purchase orders

### G. Compliance & Risk

**Time estimate: 2–4 hours**

- [ ] GST registration (see Financial section)

- [ ] Battery/lithium shipping compliance:
  - GPUs with VRAM don't contain batteries – generally not restricted
  - If selling battery-containing products (power banks, laptops): Must comply with **UN38.3** and IATA Packing Instructions
  - Australia Post: Check Dangerous Goods guide at auspost.com.au/business
  - For air freight to Malaysia (Phase 2): Requires dangerous goods declaration
  - First step: Check each product's SDS (Safety Data Sheet); keep on file

- [ ] Data privacy (Australian Privacy Act 1988):
  - Must have a Privacy Policy on your website (done in legal pages above)
  - Must inform customers what data you collect and why
  - Cannot sell customer data to third parties without consent
  - GDPR note: If selling to EU customers, add GDPR consent banner (Shopify Cookie Consent Banner app)
  - First step: Add Privacy Policy to Shopify footer; ensure Klaviyo opt-in is double opt-in

- [ ] Prohibited products check:
  - Ensure all products comply with ACMA (Australian Communications and Media Authority) labelling
  - Products with WiFi/Bluetooth require ACMA compliance (RCM mark)
  - Sourcing from Australian distributors (Dicker Data, Synnex) ensures this is already handled

- [ ] ACCC competition law:
  - No misleading claims: "Cheapest in Australia" requires proof
  - No fake reviews: Do not incentivise positive-only reviews (FTC/ACCC guidelines)
  - Price comparison: Must compare to genuine prior price

### H. Phase 2/3 Plans (Malaysia 4PL & Regional Scale)

**Time estimate: 5–10 hours research; 3–6 months to execute**

- [ ] Malaysia 4PL shortlist and contact:
  - **AFM Fulfillment** (Shah Alam, Selangor): https://afmfulfillment.com – email quotes@afmfulfillment.com
  - **MMAG Logistics** (Kuala Lumpur): https://mmag.com.my – request RFQ
  - **JD Logistics MY**: https://www.jdl.com.my – enterprise-level; approach in Phase 3
  - Request quote template: "We are an Australian e-commerce business seeking 4PL services for 500–2000 units/month. Please quote for: receiving, QC, storage, pick-pack, last-mile AU/SG/MY."

- [ ] SIRIM certification timeline (for Malaysia):
  - SIRIM is Malaysia's standards body; required for electrical products sold in Malaysia
  - Lead time: 3–6 months per product category
  - Cost: ~RM3,000–8,000 per product (AUD ~$900–2,400)
  - First step: Visit sirim.my; identify applicable standards for your SKUs (e.g., MS IEC 60950)
  - Start this process at Month 9–10 to be ready for Month 13 regionalisation

- [ ] IMDA certification (Singapore):
  - IMDA regulates communications equipment in Singapore
  - Most ACMA-compliant (RCM marked) products are accepted
  - First step: https://www.imda.gov.sg/regulations-and-licensing-listing/regulated-equipment

- [ ] Freight forwarder quotes (China → Malaysia):
  - Contact 3 forwarders: Flexport, Sino Shipping, Shipa Freight
  - Request FCL (Full Container Load) and LCL (Less than Container Load) quotes
  - Target: < $1.50/kg all-in for Shenzhen → Port Klang
  - First step: Get quotes from flexport.com (online RFQ tool)

- [ ] Chinese manufacturer vetting checklist:
  - [ ] Request factory audit report (SGS or Bureau Veritas)
  - [ ] Verify BSCI or ISO 9001 certification
  - [ ] Order minimum 2 samples before bulk order
  - [ ] Review product liability insurance coverage
  - [ ] Negotiate escrow payment (Alibaba Trade Assurance for first orders)
  - [ ] Confirm RCM/ACMA compliance documentation
